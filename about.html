    The Long Version

    I'd rather take something apart, mess with it, and learn how to fix it than just leave it alone.

    I'm a networking student who's really into technology, computers, and figuring out how things work. Networking, cybersecurity, homelabs, and PC building are what I care about most, basically anything that lets me actually work with technology instead of just reading about it.

    I've picked up experience through internships, school projects, and messing with different kinds of tech on my own. I've worked with WordPress, networking equipment, Python scripts, servers, and troubleshooting problems that don't always have an obvious answer. I'm still learning, and I like that part of it. I'd rather actually understand something than pretend I already know it.

    How I got into networking and security

    I picked the Computer Networking & Security program at Monroe Career & Technical Institute because I wanted to actually work with this stuff instead of just reading about it. Learning networking first is what made security click: addressing, subnetting, switching, routing, all of it. TestOut PC Pro and the Cisco courses gave me a real sense of how traffic moves and what shouldn't be allowed to follow it.

    Competing

    CyberPatriot is what really got me hooked. You get a broken Windows or Linux box and a clock, and you have to find the misconfigurations and bad accounts before time runs out. No partial credit for knowing the theory. My team took second place, gold tier, at the CyberPatriot XVIII Pennsylvania state round. SkillsUSA added the troubleshooting side, I placed second in IT Servicing at districts.

    Internships and AI

    My internships at Achyuraa put me on the AI and machine learning side, which I didn't expect to get into as much as I did. I built a prototype to catch AI hallucinations, cleaned and structured datasets, and benchmarked different models against each other. The next summer I worked on their customer facing chatbot, organizing the FAQ system and writing the WordPress guide for deploying it.

    Outside of school

    I build PCs, mess with my homelab, game, and listen to a lot of music. I've also gotten into buying and reselling electronics and computers. I like the idea of making something out of tech that other people overlook.

    Where I'm headed

    I don't have everything figured out yet. I want to keep working with networking and security, keep building out my homelab, and eventually do something with technology that I actually enjoy for a living. Right now that means an internship or entry role where I can work with real infrastructure: help desk, network operations, or a security team.

automatically, no code editing needed.

Tips:
  - JPG or PNG both work.
  - Any size is fine, but if your photos are huge (multiple MB, straight off
    a phone), the pages will load slower. Around 1200px wide is plenty.
  - Keep the filenames exactly as spelled above (all lowercase, with dashes),
    or the photo won't show up.
  - Until you add a photo, the browser shows its usual broken-image icon
    plus the caption below it. That's expected, not a bug.

Want to use different names instead? Open index.html and change the "src"
attributes in the matching spots.
